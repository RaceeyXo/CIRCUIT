The problem

Shared budgets break when agents act at once

Each agent checks the balance, sees room, and pays. Run them concurrently and they all see the same balance. Delegation makes it worse: sub-agents hand out more than they got, revoked agents keep paying, spend is split to dodge vendor caps.

Budgets that nest

Delegation carves budget out of the parent, so nothing is counted twice. Up to four levels; a parent's limits bind its whole branch.

Per transaction≤ 1.0
Pending total≤ 10.0
Per counterparty, whole branch≤ 6.0
Reservation lifetime≤ 24 h

Limits that can't be split around

Counterparty exposure is tracked for every ancestor, so spreading payments across children never gets past a branch cap.
